Pro Engineer — sim racing telemetry and a virtual race engineer

It reads your telemetry and tells you what to change.

Most telemetry tools hand you graphs and leave the interpreting to you. This one does the interpreting — "fronts over 28.4 psi, take 0.3 out" rather than a line on a chart — in a fast terminal on your second screen and inside the game. Free, open source, no account, and nothing about you leaves your machine.

● High

Several observations agreeing closely — four corners saying the same thing, not one odd frame.

◐ Medium

Enough to raise. Not enough to bet a setup change on.

○ Low

It is watching and has not decided. Being told that is worth more than a confident guess.

PICK YOUR SIMULATOR

A simulator is a folder under core/src/games/ rather than conditionals through the middle of the engineer, so a second one is an addition and not a rewrite. One is written. It is said that way on purpose: not yet rather than coming soon.
