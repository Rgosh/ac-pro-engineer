# Drift and street runs

`drift.rs`, `street.rs`.

**Drift** — the angle, the drift in progress with its points, combo and time,
the session's best and total, spins, and how each drift ended. After five
drifts the coach says what to work on: angle, speed, transitions or line.

**Street / traffic runs** — the No Hesi kind of evening: speed, the run in
progress and its multiplier, the best run, the longest clean distance, top
speed and contacts.

**The engineer stays quiet while you slide.** When the driving reads as a
drift, grip advice — "oversteer, soften the rear" — would be advice against
what the driver is doing on purpose, so it is held back.
